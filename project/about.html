Gear & Maintenance Tips

Fixing a Flat Tire on the Trail

Always carry a spare tube, tire levers, and a mini-pump or CO2 inflator. Pry one bead of the tire off the rim, pull out the punctured tube, check the inside of the tire for thorns or glass, insert the new tube partially inflated, and seat the bead back onto the rim.

Indexing Your Gears

If your chain skips or hesitates when shifting, use the barrel adjuster on your rear derailleur. Turn it counter-clockwise (left) to add cable tension if the chain struggles to shift to larger cogs, or clockwise (right) to ease tension if it struggles to drop to smaller cogs.
